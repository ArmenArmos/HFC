# Armenia POC: decisions for the clinical review

Status: pending clinician review. This is a discussion sheet, not an approved protocol.

Use three fictional stories: routine follow-up (Aram), further evaluation (Mariam), and urgent symptoms (Levon). Walk through each as the member, doctor and coordinator. Confirm the intended action, owner, timing and evidence needed at every handoff.

| Decision | What the demonstration currently does | What the clinical team must confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent escalation | Shows source-based urgent guidance and pauses routine care. Keeps an incident open across later readings. No real notification is sent. | Who receives each alert, coverage hours, response deadline, backup owner, and escalation if nobody responds. |
| Acknowledgement and closure | Demo doctor acknowledges, records an outcome, and closes only after current evidence is non-urgent and required symptom answers are present. Earlier plans then need republishing. | Required contact/assessment, evidence, permitted dispositions, follow-up interval, and whether any incident can ever be resolved remotely. A lower reading alone must not be interpreted as clinical clearance. |
| Test selection | Rules propose investigations with source references; demo doctor edits and approves the set. | Indications, exclusions, local test availability, preparation, referral route, and any incomplete or disputed source branches. |
| Results and risk | Displays laboratory values, supported rule findings and missing inputs. Conflicting evidence blocks publication. Does not finalize cardiovascular risk or treatment. | Structured eGFR, urine ACR, ECG/echo and organ-damage inputs; required exclusion checks; region-appropriate SCORE2 approach; diabetes confirmation; external-source validation. |
| Action plans | Doctor supplies each action, responsible role and due date. Completion is an explicit demo record. | Wording appropriate for members, medication/prescribing boundaries, clinical targets and follow-up timing. No automatic clinical deadlines are currently generated. |
| Monitoring | Simulates device readings and separates reported intake from pack-opening events. | Measurement protocol, minimum useful observation period, adherence interpretation and missed-measurement follow-up. |

For every unresolved item, record the clinical owner, decision, source/version and review date. Until these decisions and the operational integrations are approved, this remains a demonstration with fictional downstream care, not a clinical service.
